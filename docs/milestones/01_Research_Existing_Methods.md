# Milestone 1 — Research Existing Methods

## Objective
Study traditional authentication methods and the limitations of password-only security.

## Work Completed
- Password-based authentication reviewed.
- OTP and multi-factor authentication reviewed.
- Knowledge-based, possession-based, and biometric factors compared.
- Main password limitations identified: theft, phishing, reuse, weak passwords, credential stuffing.

## Short Comparison
| Method | Advantage | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Password | Cheap and common | Can be stolen/guessed/reused |
| OTP | Adds possession factor | SIM/email compromise, extra friction |
| Fingerprint/Face | Strong biometric signal | Requires sensor/device support and privacy handling |
| Behavioral biometrics | Passive, no special hardware | Behavior varies and may cause false rejections |
